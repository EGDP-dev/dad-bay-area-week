Family time in Burlingame

Slow days close to home

Not every day needs to be a tour. These are all within about 15 minutes of our house in Burlingame, about 25 minutes down 101 from his hotel. Put one or two on any day the planner marks as full, or make a whole day of them.

The 8-day plan

Day 1 is his late-evening arrival and Day 8 is the trip home, so the plan fills Days 2–7. Drive times are estimates from his hotel on Van Ness without heavy traffic. A day turns amber past about 5 hours and red past 9.

His hotel

Holiday Inn San Francisco – Golden Gateway, 1500 Van Ness Ave (Nob Hill / Polk Village). On the California St cable car line, steps from Polk Street cafés. Parking $49/night with in-and-out; the garage height limit is 5′10″.

This is a sample plan built around opening days: Cotogna and Verjus on Thursday, Golden Gate Park on Friday (free Tea Garden hour, everything open), Marin on Saturday for weekend brunch and the Bay Model, family on Sunday. Move anything; changes save automatically.
